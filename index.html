Play solo, in small teams, or together on a projector. Everyone gets six guesses per word and four mistakes per Connections board. Start with the three Connections boards, then play eight word puzzles. Suggested party time: 15–25 minutes. On a shared screen, nominate one person to enter the team’s guesses.

Personnel categories use the names supplied for this event. These boards blend cost analysis, economics, and surname wordplay. A surname may return on another board with a different connection. Answers are contained in this offline file, so this is an honor-system game.
